**1. Introduction** \\

**1.1 Purpose**
This Software Requirement Specification (SRS) document outlines the requirements for developing “Medibuddy” - an all-in-one health record and telemedicine service based web application using a custom python based tech stack. The primary goal of this application is to help long term patients keep all their medical records in one place for easy access and management. This also allows doctors to view patients records as far back as available without the risk of patients being unable to recall their medical history; thus streamlining the diagnosis process and reducing the risk of maltreatment due to negligence resulting from the omitting of critical health information during the diagnosis process. It has some telemedicine features such as video conference facilities to further help patients get the proper treatment they deserve. The privacy and information-security of patients is ensured as well. \\

**1.2 Scope**
The scope of this project includes the design, development, testing, and deployment of the web application, catering to:
Patients who can register, book appointments, upload and archive electronic medical records, and receive telemedicine services.
Doctors who can manage appointments, conduct telemedicine sessions, access patient records with their permission, and make prescriptions.
Administrators who oversee system operations, manage user roles, receive user complaints and queries, and maintain compliance standards.
